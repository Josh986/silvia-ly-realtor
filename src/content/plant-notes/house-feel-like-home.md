People often think making a house feel like home requires big projects. In my experience, it's the small rituals that matter most.

It might be a plant on the kitchen windowsill, a favorite chair placed by the best light, or the smell of something cooking on the first night. These little touches settle you in far faster than any renovation.

Give yourself permission to take your time. A home grows into itself — and so do you.
