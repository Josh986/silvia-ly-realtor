When you move into your first home, adding a plant or two is one of the easiest ways to make the space feel like yours. You don't need to be an expert — start with a few forgiving favorites.

A pothos is nearly impossible to kill and happy almost anywhere. A snake plant thrives on neglect and tolerates low light. A ZZ plant is another sturdy companion for busy new homeowners.

Give them a little water, a little light, and a little patience — much like settling into a new home.
