Before I even look at the square footage, I find myself noticing how the light moves through a room. A home with good natural light feels different from the first moment you walk in — softer, warmer, more alive.

I like to visit a home at different times of day when I can. Morning light in a breakfast nook, afternoon sun in a living room, the way a west-facing window glows near sunset — these small things tell you how it will feel to actually live there.

If you're a plant person like me, light is everything. A bright, south-facing windowsill can become your favorite corner of the house.
